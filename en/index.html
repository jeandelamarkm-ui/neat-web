Friction you learned to live with.

It rarely shows up as one dramatic failure. It accumulates as the business grows, until keeping the operation running starts taking capacity away from the business itself.

The same information, again

Data moves from email to spreadsheets to another system, then gets entered all over again.

Approvals you have to chase

Requests sit in an inbox and nobody is quite sure where they stand.

A process that lives in one person

If that person is out, work slows down or someone has to reconstruct the process.

Rework and exceptions

Errors get fixed by hand, again and again.

Opportunities left waiting

The NEAT Protocol

See what was already there. Connect what was missing. Measure what changed.

Our way of moving from operational friction to a system that works in the real flow of work.

  1. Understand

    You get a shared map of the problem and a baseline when one exists.

  2. Find

    You get priorities based on business criteria, not a list of AI ideas.

  3. Design

    You get scope, future flow, owners, acceptance criteria and a decision to build—or not.

  4. Implement

    You get an accepted system, a named owner, documentation and agreed support.

  5. Measure & evolve

    You get evidence of the change and a clear next decision.
