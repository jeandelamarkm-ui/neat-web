01 · Healthcare · Pharma operations

Medication should arrive on time without someone chasing the order.

The friction
Orders and deliveries needed better visibility, traceability, and earlier intervention.
The system
We built a dispatch platform with real-time tracking, digital proof of delivery, an interactive dashboard by order and region, and intelligent monitoring that flags deliveries at risk.
What changed
Less manual follow-up. More visibility. Issues surfaced before they became complaints.

02 · Field services · Sales operations

No customer left waiting.

The friction
WhatsApp and email scattered conversations, owners, and next steps.
The system
We connected the channels to the CRM so every conversation is created, assigned, and tracked automatically. An agent monitors response times and alerts the team lead when something is left unattended.
What changed
Every request has an owner. Follow-up no longer depends on copy, paste, or memory.

03 · Telecom · Sales

AI handles the first touch. People step in when it matters.

The friction
Sales reps were spending time from the first contact, even when the opportunity was not qualified yet.
The system
We implemented a voice agent that answers and profiles callers. During the call, an AI copilot transcribes, suggests next steps, and analyzes signals in the conversation. Supervisors see activity and opportunities in real time.
What changed
Sales reps get conversations with more context. Supervisors see quality, volume, and opportunities as they happen.
