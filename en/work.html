Case structure.

01

Situation

The process context and why it mattered.

02

Friction

Where time, capacity, control or money was being lost.

03

Intervention

What NEAT changed, with which people and systems.

04

Evidence

What improved, over what period and against which source or baseline.

05

What it unlocked

What the team or business could now do that was harder before.

The proof is not “we used AI.” The proof is that the work changed and we can explain how.
