The NEAT Protocol

Understand what slows you down. Design what comes next. Measure what changed.

We do not start with technology. We start with the real work: how information moves, where it gets stuck, who decides and what would have to change for the business to gain capacity.

Our own way of understanding, prioritizing, building, adopting and measuring a transformation.

Your business, your people and how work actually gets done. We observe the process, shortcuts, dependencies, exceptions and owners.

You get A shared map of the problem and a baseline when one exists.

We identify where time, capacity or money is being lost and which frictions are important enough to justify intervention.

You get Priorities based on business criteria, not a list of AI ideas.

We define the simplest way to solve it: what to remove, connect, automate, keep human and how exceptions should work.

You get Scope, future flow, owners, acceptance criteria and a decision to build—or not.

We build and integrate the solution into the real operation. We test real tasks with users, document decisions and prepare adoption.

You get An accepted system, a named owner, documentation and agreed support.

We compare the outcome with the baseline, review use, quality and operational effect, then decide what to keep, improve or scale.

You get Evidence of the change and a clear next decision.

Clarity reaches the project too.

Before we promise

Business and engineering review feasibility, scope, risk and capacity together.

During the work

One person is accountable. Decisions, changes and exceptions stay visible.

At delivery

The team shows it can operate the system with the agreed documentation and controls.

After

We compare results with the baseline and agree on the next decision.

Ways to work with NEAT.

01

NEAT Triage

When the problem still needs definition. We deliver an opportunity map: priorities, feasibility, scope and a baseline to decide whether it is worth building.

02

Implementation

When there is a prioritized process. We design, build, integrate, test and support adoption of the system.

03

Ongoing care

When there is a real recurring responsibility: monitoring, support, quality or improvement within a defined scope.

04

Repeatable solutions

Only when the same problem has been validated enough times to have known compatibility, limits, support and cost.

Control and data.

Access and permissions

Defined before production and limited to what the scope requires.

Exceptions and approval

Sensitive or out-of-rule decisions are routed to the responsible person when appropriate.

Quality and logging

Each project defines what should be logged, reviewed and measured based on the risk of the process.

Data and providers

Roles, providers, data use, retention and relevant conditions are documented before production.

Ownership and licensing

Ownership of deliverables and use of reusable components are defined in writing in the contract.

Frequently asked questions.

We already use AI. Why would we need you?

Good. Let’s look at what still depends on copying, chasing, coordinating or handling exceptions by hand. Often the tool is already there; the end-to-end workflow is not.

Why not just buy a tool?

If what you already have solves it, we start there. We look at what is missing for the process to work end to end: integration, exceptions, owners and adoption.

How much will we save?

Before estimating a benefit, we agree on a baseline and the total cost. We always separate an estimate from a measured result.

Why pay every month?

Only if NEAT takes on a real recurring responsibility such as monitoring, support or improvement. What it covers is defined in writing.

Will AI replace my team?

Our job is to remove friction so your team can use its capacity where it adds more value. Before designing, we agree on what tasks change and what decisions remain human.

Do my data need to be in order?

Not necessarily. Part of the Triage is understanding how ready the operation is and what needs to be organized before we build.
