The NEAT Protocol

We don’t start with AI. We start with how your business actually runs.

Before we recommend technology, we understand the process, the cost of the friction, and what would have to change for an intervention to be worthwhile.

01 · Understand

01

See the operation as it actually runs.

People, steps, systems, exceptions, decisions, dependencies. Not the process on paper — the real one.

02 · Find

02

Not every friction point needs technology.

We prioritize opportunities with enough operational and economic upside to justify intervention.

03 · Design

03

Simplify before you automate.

We define what should run on its own, what needs a person, what systems need to connect, and how exceptions are handled.

04 · Implement

04

A system only matters if it works in the real world.

We build, connect, test, and put the solution into the operation with clear owners and acceptance criteria.

05 · Measure

05

If it changed, we should be able to prove it.

We compare against the available baseline, review adoption, and decide what is worth improving or scaling.

If automating it doesn’t make sense, we don’t.

Sometimes the best answer is to configure what you already have, redesign the process, or not build anything yet.
