About NEAT

Your company already has something too valuable to replace. The knowledge of the people who built it.

NEAT designs intelligent systems around that knowledge to remove repetitive work, connect what currently lives apart, and give capacity back to the people who actually move the business forward.

Belief

Humans where they matter most.

Technology should not make a company less human. It should take away the work that does not require human judgment so people can focus on the work that does.

We automate the repetitive. We amplify what matters.

How we think

We don’t confuse sophistication with complexity.

  • —

    If simple solves it, we choose simple.

  • —

    If what you already have works, we use it.

  • —

    If a decision needs judgment, a person stays in the loop.

  • —

    If a system cannot be controlled, it is not ready.

  • —

    If we cannot explain why it creates value, we should not build it.

Boutique by design

Fewer projects. More accountability.

We work closely with leadership and the people who actually run the process. We understand, build, test, and support adoption.
