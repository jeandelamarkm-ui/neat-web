About

A sharp mind. A calm presence.

NEAT exists to make technology remove what keeps people from adding value. We observe how an operation actually works, find the friction that became normal and build a clearer way to work.

The belief behind the way we work.

Built from Colombia. Thinking beyond it.

NEAT grew from a simple ambition: local talent should be able to build higher-value technology and help businesses stop relying on manual effort for everything. We build from Colombia and work in Spanish and English.

Humans where it really matters.

The next era of business should not feel less human because technology becomes more sophisticated. It should give people more room to decide, create, sell, connect and solve what still requires judgment.

Our values show in the work.

Judgment

We choose the problem by impact before choosing technology.

Empathy

We observe real work and test with the people who will use the system.

Clarity

We explain decisions, scope and limits in plain language.

Accountability

We agree on how to measure and separate estimates from results.

Ingenuity

We find useful connections and simplify what is left over.
